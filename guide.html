3 Golden Rules to Spot Phishing

🔍

1. Check Sender Domain

Scammers register look-alike domains with swapped letters.

✓ support@apple.com
✕ support@app1e-help.com
⏳

2. Beware of Urgency

Threats of 2-hour account deletion aim to trigger panic.

Pause and verify through official channels before acting.
🔗

3. Hover Over Links

Preview the true destination URL before clicking any button.

Never enter passwords on unencrypted http:// or bit.ly links.
